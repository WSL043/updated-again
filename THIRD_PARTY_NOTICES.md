# Third-party data

## KdConv

The local chat includes 80,063 adjacent dialog pairs derived from [thu-coai/KdConv](https://github.com/thu-coai/KdConv) commit `653db76432de09a004ba708a68f8bbd5500e6bec`.

KdConv was created by Hao Zhou, Chujie Zheng, Kaili Huang, Minlie Huang, and Xiaoyan Zhu and released under the Apache License 2.0. The unmodified license is stored at [`third_party/KdConv-LICENSE`](third_party/KdConv-LICENSE).

## ChatterBot Corpus

The local chat also includes every language pack from [gunthercox/chatterbot-corpus](https://github.com/gunthercox/chatterbot-corpus) commit `eec45b284424c9784a5baab78368b1a9ff3b656f`.

ChatterBot Corpus is released under the BSD 3-Clause License. The unmodified license is stored at [`third_party/ChatterBot-Corpus-LICENSE`](third_party/ChatterBot-Corpus-LICENSE).

## XDailyDialog

The local chat includes all English, Chinese, German, and Italian conversations from [liuzeming01/XDailyDialog](https://github.com/liuzeming01/XDailyDialog) commit `6e7ecf54c9f169215b4b8c18995c7aac74117127`.

XDailyDialog was created by Zeming Liu, Ping Nie, Jie Cai, Haifeng Wang, Zheng-Yu Niu, Peng Zhang, Mrinmaya Sachan, and Kaiping Peng and released under the Apache License 2.0. The unmodified license is stored at [`third_party/XDailyDialog-LICENSE`](third_party/XDailyDialog-LICENSE).
